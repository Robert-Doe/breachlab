CSP Directive Quick Reference

DirectiveControlsRecommended value
default-srcFallback for all resource types'self'
script-src<script> and JS event handlers'nonce-{random}' or 'strict-dynamic'
style-src<style> and style= attributes'self' (avoid 'unsafe-inline')
img-src<img> and CSS background images'self' data: (restrict for exfil prevention)
connect-srcfetch(), XHR, WebSocket, sendBeacon'self' (explicit allowlist)
frame-src<iframe> and <frame>'none' or specific origins
object-src<object>, <embed>'none'
base-uri<base> tag (base URL injection)'none' or 'self'
report-uriViolation reporting endpointSet to your logging endpoint

Avoid: 'unsafe-inline' in script-src (defeats XSS protection), 'unsafe-eval' (enables eval), wildcard * in any fetch directive.
